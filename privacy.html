The Nepali (Devanagari) version of this document, where it exists, shall prevail in case of any conflict with this English text.

01

Introduction

VisionGuard Security & Hardware ("we", "us", "our", or "the Store") is a retail and service business based at New Baneshwor, Kathmandu, Nepal. We value the privacy of our customers, visitors and trade partners. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have under Nepali law.

By visiting our store, our website, or by purchasing goods and installation services from us, you confirm that you have read and understood this Policy.

02

Scope & Legal Basis

This Policy is prepared with reference to the following laws of Nepal, which together form the legal basis for how we handle your personal information:

  • The Constitution of Nepal, 2015, Article 28 — recognising the right to privacy as a fundamental right.
  • The Individual Privacy Act, 2075 (2018) and the Individual Privacy Regulation, 2077 (2020) — the primary statutes governing collection and processing of personal information in Nepal.
  • The Electronic Transactions Act, 2063 (2008) — governing electronic records, digital communication and cyber offences.
  • The Consumer Protection Act, 2075 (2018) — protecting purchasers of goods and services.
  • The National Penal Code, 2074 (2017) — including provisions on privacy-related offences.

Nepal does not currently have a single comprehensive data protection statute equivalent to the EU GDPR, and no dedicated Data Protection Authority is presently operational. Complaints under the Individual Privacy Act are heard by the relevant District Court.

03

Information We Collect

We collect only the personal information reasonably required to serve you. Depending on how you interact with us, this may include:

a) Contact & identification details

  • Full name, telephone or WhatsApp number, email address.
  • Delivery and installation address for the supply or fitting of goods.
  • Citizenship or PAN/VAT details only where required for tax invoicing of trade or bulk orders.

b) Transaction details

  • Records of items purchased, dates of purchase, warranty registrations, and after-sales service history.
  • Payment method used (cash, bank transfer, eSewa, Khalti, IME Pay, card, etc.). We do not store full card numbers; payments are processed through licensed payment service providers.

c) Website & communication data

  • Messages submitted through our contact form or social media channels.
  • Basic technical information automatically logged by your browser (IP address, device type, pages viewed) — see section 10.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as caste, ethnicity, religion, political views, health records or biometric data, except where strictly necessary for a specific service you have requested (for example, fingerprint enrolment for a biometric lock you have purchased from us).

04

How We Use Your Information

Information collected from you is used solely for the purposes for which it was provided, including:

  • Processing your orders, preparing quotations and issuing tax invoices.
  • Arranging delivery, site survey, installation and after-sales servicing.
  • Honouring product warranties and managing repair or replacement requests.
  • Responding to enquiries received via phone, WhatsApp, email, social media or our contact form.
  • Sending occasional information about new stock, offers or service reminders — only where you have agreed to receive such messages.
  • Complying with tax, accounting and other legal obligations under the laws of Nepal.

We will not use your information for any purpose materially different from those listed above without first asking for your consent.

05

Consent

Under the Individual Privacy Act, 2075, personal information may generally be collected only after informing you of the purpose and obtaining your consent. By providing us with your details — whether at our store counter, by telephone, through our website, or through any electronic channel — you consent to our collecting and using that information for the purposes described in this Policy.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us using the details in section 14. Where consent is withdrawn, we will stop processing your information for the affected purpose, except where we are required to retain it under Nepali law (for example, tax records).

06

Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only in the following limited situations:

  • Service partners — delivery couriers, installation sub-contractors, manufacturer service centres (for warranty claims) and licensed payment processors. These partners receive only the minimum information needed to perform their function.
  • Accountants & auditors — for the purpose of bookkeeping, tax filing and statutory audit.
  • Lawful requests — where we are required to disclose information by a competent authority of Nepal, including a court order, a written request from an investigating authority under the Criminal Procedure Code, 2074 or other prevailing laws.
  • Business protection — where reasonably necessary to investigate, prevent or take action regarding suspected fraud, theft, threats to physical safety, or violations of our Terms of Service.

We do not transfer your personal information abroad except where required by an overseas manufacturer to honour a warranty claim, or where information is hosted by an international service provider (for example, cloud-based email or analytics). In such cases, we take reasonable steps to ensure your information continues to be protected.

07

Data Storage & Security

Customer records are stored at our office in Kathmandu in both physical form (printed invoices, registers) and electronic form (computer records and accounting software). Electronic records are protected with access controls, password protection and regular backups.

We make reasonable arrangements, as required by Section 27 of the Individual Privacy Act, 2075, to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet and no method of electronic storage is fully secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we will act promptly to investigate and address any incident that may affect your information.

08

Retention Period

We retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or for as long as we are required to retain it under Nepali law. As a general practice:

  • Invoicing and tax records are retained for a minimum of five (5) years as required under the Income Tax Act, 2058 and applicable VAT rules.
  • Warranty and installation records are retained for the duration of the warranty period plus a reasonable additional period for service follow-up.
  • Marketing contact details are retained only while you remain subscribed.

After the relevant period, records are securely deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

09

Your Rights

Subject to the limits of Nepali law, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about what personal information of yours we hold and the purpose for which it is held.
  • Request access to your personal information and request correction of any inaccuracies.
  • Withdraw your consent to further processing, except where we have a legal obligation to continue holding the information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to the retention obligations described in section 8.
  • Object to receiving promotional messages from us at any time.
  • File a complaint with the appropriate District Court in the event of a serious privacy violation, as provided under the Individual Privacy Act, 2075.

Requests of this kind can be made using the contact details in section 14. We will respond within a reasonable period, ordinarily not exceeding fifteen (15) working days.

10

Cookies & Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, count page visits and improve site performance. Cookies are small text files placed on your device by your browser. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or to delete them at any time through your browser settings.

Where cookies or analytics tools collect information that could identify you personally, the consent requirements of the Individual Privacy Act, 2075 apply, and you may withdraw consent by disabling cookies in your browser. Refusing cookies will not prevent you from browsing our site, although some features may not work as intended.

11

CCTV Footage Captured at Our Premises

For the safety of customers, staff and stock, CCTV cameras are operated at our store and warehouse. Notices are displayed at the entrances to inform you that filming is taking place. Footage is stored on a secure recorder, accessed only by authorised staff, and ordinarily over-written within thirty (30) days unless retained for a specific incident or as required by the Nepal Police or a competent authority.

12

Children's Privacy

Our products and services are directed at adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from any person below the age of eighteen (18) without the consent of a parent or legal guardian. If you believe a minor has provided us with information without such consent, please contact us and we will delete it.

13

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or in the law (for example, the proposed Information Technology and Cyber Security Bill currently before the House of Representatives). The latest version will always be posted on this page, with the "Last Updated" date shown at the top.

14

Contact & Complaints

Questions, requests or complaints about this Policy or about your personal information can be sent to us at the address shown in the box below. If your concern is not resolved to your satisfaction, you have the right to seek remedy through the relevant District Court under the Individual Privacy Act, 2075.
